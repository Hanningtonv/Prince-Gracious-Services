Our Professional Services

Explore what Prince-Gracious Services offers for your home and workplace, backed by advanced machinery.

Office Cleaning

Comprehensive workspace cleaning, desk sanitization, glass cleaning, and floor care.

Equipment & Tech Used:

  • Commercial HEPA Dry Vacuum Cleaners (Numatic Henry / Kärcher T-Series): High-filtration to trap fine dust and allergens without disrupting working staff.
  • Walk-Behind Automatic Floor Scrubbers (Kärcher BD Series / Viper): Heavy-duty machines that scrub, wash, and dry reception floors in a single pass.
  • Single-Disc Floor Polishers & Buffers: High-speed rotary units to restore shine to marble, granite, and PVC floors.

Kitchen Cleaning

Deep grease removal, appliance degreasing, countertop sanitizing, and hygiene restoration.

Equipment & Tech Used:

  • Commercial High-Temperature Steam Cleaners (Kärcher SG Series): Chemical-free, pressurized steam units that dissolve baked-on grease and sanitize tile grout.
  • Industrial Wet & Dry Vacuum Extractors (Kärcher NT Series / Bosch GAS): Stainless-steel units built to safely extract heavy grease water and liquid spills.

Carpet Cleaning

Deep steam extraction, stain removal, and odor elimination for carpets and rugs.

Equipment & Tech Used:

  • Spray-Extraction Carpet & Sofa Cleaners (Kärcher Puzzi Series): Hot-water injection and extraction machines that flush out deep-seated dirt and dust mites.
  • Rotary Carpet Scrubbers: Low-speed agitation machines to loosen heavy traffic stains before extraction.
  • High-Velocity Air Movers (Carpet Dryers): Industrial blowers used to accelerate drying times and prevent damp odors.

Fumigation

Safe and efficient pest control targeting bedbugs, cockroaches, termites, and rodents.

Equipment & Tech Used:

  • ULV (Ultra-Low Volume) Cold Foggers: Electric misting machines that disperse microscopic droplets of insecticide safely across indoor spaces.
  • Thermal Fogging Machines: Heavy-duty outdoor foggers that generate dense pest-control smoke for gardens and perimeters.
  • Pressurized Residual Knapsack Sprayers: Precision sprayers for applying protective barriers along skirting boards and wall cracks.

General House Cleaning

Complete residential cleaning including living rooms, bedrooms, bathrooms, and balconies.

Equipment & Tech Used:

  • Multi-Surface Canister Vacuums: Lightweight, high-suction vacuums equipped with attachments for curtains, mattresses, and tight corners.
  • Portable Upholstery Spot Extractors: Compact deep-cleaning units tailored for targeted stain removal on dining chairs and mattresses.
  • Electric High-Pressure Washers (Kärcher K-Series / Bosch Aquatak): Used to blast away moss and grime from driveways, verandas, and water tanks.
